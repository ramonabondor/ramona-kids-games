Questions from parents

Where can we download the apps?+

They’re still in development. You can explore the previews here, and we’ll add official download links when the apps are ready.

Which app should we start with?+

Try PuzzlesKids for matching at ages 2–4, Little World of Discovery for activities at ages 2–6, or Colorland Adventures for coloring at ages 3–7. Animated Stories is for families to set up and read together.

How does Animated Stories work?+

A grown-up chooses up to four characters and a theme to create a 6–8 page illustrated storybook. Books are kept in a private library and can be saved as PDFs. Optional MP4 video export depends on the final release setup.

Can we get in touch?+

Yes. Send questions about any of the apps to bondorramona17@gmail.com.
